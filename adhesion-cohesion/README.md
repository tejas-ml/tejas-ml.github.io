# Adhesion & Cohesion Simulator

**Live Demo:** [https://tejas-ml.github.io/adhesion-cohesion/](https://tejas-ml.github.io/adhesion-cohesion/)

An interactive Biology 101 learning module that lets you visualize and manipulate cohesion, adhesion, meniscus formation, and capillary action in real time. Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no dependencies.

---

## What is Cohesion?

**Cohesion** is the attraction between molecules of the *same* substance. In water, molecules are polar (one positive end, one negative end), so they form **hydrogen bonds** with each other. This is why:

- Water forms droplets instead of spreading out completely
- The surface of water acts like a thin "skin" (surface tension), letting insects like water striders walk on it

## What is Adhesion?

**Adhesion** is the attraction between molecules of *different* substances. Water sticks to polar or charged surfaces like glass, cotton, and plant tissue. This is why:

- Water climbs the walls of a glass tube, forming a curved **meniscus** (always read a graduated cylinder at the bottom of the curve)
- Paper towels absorb water
- Water moves through soil

## How They Work Together

When cohesion and adhesion combine, they produce **capillary action** — the ability of water to move upward through a narrow tube against gravity. Adhesion pulls water up the walls; cohesion drags the rest of the water column along behind it. Narrower tubes = higher rise.

This is how giant trees like redwoods move water from roots to canopy hundreds of feet up, with no pump — a process called **transpirational pull**.

## Features

- **Macro view** — watch the meniscus curve and capillary rise respond to sliders in real time
- **Micro view** — see individual water molecules and their hydrogen bonds
- **Fluid presets** — Water (concave meniscus) vs Mercury (convex meniscus)
