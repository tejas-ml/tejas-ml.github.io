How the circuit adds

Full adder

One bit of A, one bit of B, and a carry-in. XOR computes the sum bit; AND and OR compute the carry-out.

S = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin

Generate & propagate

A ∧ B generates a carry regardless of Cin. A ⊕ B propagates an incoming carry to the next bit.

Cout = (A ∧ B) ∨ (Cin ∧ (A ⊕ B))

Ripple

Cout of bit i is Cin of bit i+1. The carry must travel the whole chain, which is why this adder is simple — and slow in silicon.

C0 = 0
