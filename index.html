About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Central Florida, working in the Computational Biology Lab at the intersection of computational research and software engineering. My academic interests span single-cell multi-omics, quantitative methods, high-performance computing, and machine learning applied to scientific problems.

Alongside research, I build reliable, efficient software — from systems-level C++ and Rust libraries to full-stack web applications, AI privacy/security tools, and quantitative finance simulators. I am drawn to problems that demand both mathematical rigor and clean engineering.
