From the Arrhenius factor

01

Bind

The substrate drifts into a complementary pocket. Induced fit closes the enzyme around it, forming the enzyme–substrate complex. The protein is a catalyst: it is regenerated at the end.

02

Lower the hill

The enzyme stabilizes the transition state, so the free-energy peak drops. A smaller Ea means a far larger fraction of collisions have enough energy to react. ΔG, the net drop from reactants to products, stays the same.

03

Release

The strained bond rearranges, products leave the pocket, and the free enzyme is ready for another turnover. Watch the marble roll a lower curve when the catalyst is bound.

Illustrative landscape, not a fitted potential. Real enzymes often accelerate reactions by 106–1012. Space runs the reaction · E toggles the enzyme.
