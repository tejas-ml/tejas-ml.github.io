# Enzyme

An interactive catalysis simulator: an enzyme binds its substrate, the activation-energy curve drops, and the reaction turns over without consuming the catalyst.

Live site: [tejas-ml.github.io/enzyme](https://tejas-ml.github.io/enzyme/)

## What it shows

- **Active site** — substrate approach, induced fit, transition-state glow, product release
- **Free-energy diagram** — uncatalyzed peak vs. the lowered catalyzed path, with ΔG unchanged
- **Readouts** — *E*<sub>a</sub>, barrier drop, ΔG, and an Arrhenius relative rate

Toggle **Enzyme bound** to watch the activation curve fall. Press `Space` to run, `E` to toggle the enzyme.

Energy values are illustrative, not a fitted potential.
